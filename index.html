03 / Approach

“He was not only writing code that worked, but also thinking critically about software architecture, algorithmic design, data quality, and reproducibility.”

— Dr. Julia-Maria Hübner, TU Dresden

My work lives where scientific questions meet engineered systems. I learn the domain, find the structure inside the complexity, and build tools that let researchers move faster without sacrificing rigor.

At Carnegie Science’s Earth and Planets Laboratory, I collaborate across mineralogy, crystallography, geoscience, and planetary science—translating research questions into reliable, interpretable software.

I’m also drawn to astronomy, astrobiology, and other sciences where computation can reveal patterns that are difficult to see by observation alone. The domain may change; the goal stays the same: make ambitious research more tractable.
